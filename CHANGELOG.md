# UltimateCraft – changelog

## v5.1.0 – "alive" update: creatures, sounds, textures and physics
Textures and skins are still drawn by the game's own code, and sounds are now real recordings; this release makes the game far more convincing
and makes the world obey more of the rules you would expect.

- **Mob skins redrawn.** The skin painter now lights every face of every model box (bright tops, dark undersides),
  darkens edges for volume, and gives each creature a material: streaked fur, curly wool, feather scallops, fish and reptile
  scales, rotting cloth on the undead. Animals get lighter bellies and dark hooves, muzzles with nostrils, and real faces
  (brows, whites and pupils, mouths, teeth, creeper and spider faces). Cow and mooshroom patches are irregular
  blotches instead of a grid. The zombie is now painted like every other mob; the third-party skin that used to be copied
  in (`tools/base/zombie.png`) is gone.
- **Block textures redrawn.** New tileable fractal noise (`tnoise` in `tools/content/paint.py`) gives stone veins and
  fissures, real cobblestone cells, pebbled dirt, rippled sand, rounded gravel stones, grass blades, planks with grain,
  seams and knots, ridged bark and ringed log ends, light-catching leaves, mortar-lit bricks, banded deepslate, glinting
  ore, woven wool, and water and lava that loop seamlessly over their 16 animation frames.
- **Real sounds** (57 synthesised sounds → 171 recordings, ~470 takes). All sound effects and creature voices are now built
  from real recordings that are public domain, CC0 or CC BY (credits: `tools/audio_src/CREDITS.md`): farm animals, a cat, a horse, a
  wolf, bats, a bumblebee, a frog, a sparrow, thunder, rain, splashes and a forge fire from Wikimedia Commons; foley (footsteps,
  impacts, doors, chests, glass, metal) from Kenney's CC0 packs; monsters designed from CC0 voice recordings (transposed,
  layered, reverb). Every one of the 35 creature voice kinds has an idle, hurt and death sound, and sounds with several takes
  (footsteps, calls) pick one at random. New `tools/build_audio.py` + `tools/audio_recipes*.py` rebuild `res/audio/*.ogg` from the
  sources in `tools/audio_src/`; new `lib/stb/stb_vorbis.c` (public domain) decodes them. The old synthesiser stays as an
  automatic fallback when a file is missing. Distant sounds are muffled by distance-based air absorption; subtitles cover
  everything. New sounds for player hurt/death, doors, chests, levers, pressure plates, pistons and landing anvils.
- **Deterministic tests**: scripted runs use virtual time (one frame = one tick) and a fixed random seed instead of the wall
  clock. The two scripts that failed about once in 10-25 runs (`sculk`, `pathfinding`) had one more hidden cause, found and fixed
  afterwards: chunks were generated by worker threads and *applied* in whichever order and on whichever frame the threads
  happened to finish, and applying a chunk spawns animals and fills loot, i.e. draws from the shared random generator. A
  busy machine therefore shifted every later random roll. Scripted runs now wait for every queued chunk, apply them in
  coordinate order, mesh without a time budget and no longer depend on window focus (`g_world_sync`). Result: `sculk` and
  `pathfinding` passed 60/60 runs on a deliberately overloaded machine, and the full suite passes. New `itemx` check; new tests
  `falling` (incl. anvil damage), `trees` (incl. persistent leaves) and `fluids`.
- **Sound mastering**: the build pipeline now removes DC offset, brings every take of a sound to the same audible loudness
  (they differed by up to 8 dB), applies a soft limiter and re-encodes any file whose decoded peak would still clip. The
  chicken calls, lever and chest sounds used to clip; none of the 472 files does now. Measured, not listened to - see
  `tools/sound_lab.py` and the README.
- **More texture upgrades**: bevelled polished/chiselled blocks, grouted tiles, brushed metal, faceted gem blocks, rippled
  concrete powder, banded terracotta, veined ice; the animated water and lava were retuned.
- **README artwork** drawn as pixel-art SVGs by `tools/make_banner.py`, plus a "System requirements" section with measured memory
  and frame rates.
- **Falling blocks are real entities** (`ENT_FALLING`): sand, gravel, concrete powder and anvils accelerate, sink through
  water, are drawn in flight, land as blocks, are saved with the world, and anvils hurt what they land on.
- **Fluids**: currents now push the player, mobs and dropped items (waterfalls pull things down); lava randomly ignites
  adjacent flammable blocks. (Previously only boats felt currents.)
- **Leaf decay** uses the real connected-distance rule (a log within 6 blocks through connected leaves) instead of
  "any log within a diamond", so leaves near a different tree's trunk no longer keep a felled tree alive. Leaves placed by a player are now
  **persistent** (a second block state per leaf kind: 3,497 → 3,509 block states), so hedges and builds no longer rot away.
- **Repository**: no third-party art remains (`tools/base/` removed); `.gitattributes`, issue and pull-request templates,
  `CONTRIBUTING.md`, `SECURITY.md`; version and statistics corrected across the docs. New tests `falling`, `trees`, `fluids`;
  new tools `tools/sound_lab.py` and `tools/screenshots/v5_1.txt`. The game builds and passes its tests on Linux.

## v5.0.0 – "own world" update, part 2 (the road to the Sift)
The progression now runs **Overworld → Underground City → Space → the Sift**.

- **Zorvath, the Cosmic Tyrant** (`cosmic_tyrant`) replaces the dragon as Space's boss: an original crowned colossus
  with a halo of star shards (own model and skin). Dormant until a player comes within 96 blocks (or it is hurt),
  then three phases switched at 66 % / 33 % health, each announced with a `/title`: star-bolt volleys (new
  `STAR_BOLT` projectile, a new `starfire` death message), Star Crystal healing (phase I), gravity pulls and Void
  Drifter minions (phase II), five-bolt volleys, meteor rain and crawler minions (phase III), plus contact damage
  and a shockwave at each phase change. Gold boss bar showing the phase. Health/damage ×0.75 / ×1 / ×1.5 on
  Easy / Normal / Hard. Phase and timers persist in `entities.dat`, so the fight survives save/reload.
  On death: a 6 s collapse, 15,000 XP (1,000 afterwards), the **Tyrant's Crown** (trophy helmet, 4 armour), the
  **Sift Key**, nether stars and stardust; the exit portal opens and a gold **Sift gateway** rises on the central
  island. `level.txt` gains `tyrant_killed` and `sift_gate`. `/siftgate` teleports to the gateway.
- **Space**: new mobs **Void Drifter** (hovers above you, dives, sting = brief levitation) and **Asteroid Crawler**
  (armoured rock arthropod), both spawning on the outer asteroids; **Oxygen Bloom** plant (generated on outer
  asteroids, glows, refills air within 3 blocks); **Oxygen Canister** (3 minutes of Oxygen, new `OXYGEN` item use);
  **Stardust**. The starfield has bigger, brighter stars and a few glowing giants.
- **The Underground City** (`underground_city`, aliases `delvari_city`, `city`): a ~120-block walled city in a
  domed cavern at y −48, built from modular pieces on a 7×7 grid (keep, market, temple, prison, barracks, houses,
  plazas, gardens, ruins), lit by **Umbral Lamps**, with new blocks (Delvari Bricks/Tiles/Pillar, Runic Delvari
  Bricks), three loot tables, **Delvari Merchants** (new trading profession, paid in **Delvari Coins**), **Hollow
  Sentinels** and the mini-boss **Morrowgate, Keeper of the Deep** (ground slam, lantern fire, phase II summons).
  He drops the **Void Sigil** and sometimes the **Umbral Blade**. `/locate underground_city [tp]` works.
- **The Space portal now needs a Void Sigil**: a full ring of Star Eyes only opens if you carry a sigil (it is
  consumed), or when you right-click the full frame with one. Creative mode is exempt.
- **Ender re-theme** (display names, models, colours; ids unchanged): Voidwalker (horns, starry indigo skin),
  Void Mite, Void Wyrm, Star Eye, Void Pearl, Void Chest, Wyrm's Breath/Egg/Head, Starshell (+ Starshell Box /
  Plate), Nebulite, Starbloom. New mob skin pattern `stars`.
- **The Sift, part 2**: **Wandering Souls** (translucent, talk for lore, a one-time blessing + Soul Essence, sneak-click
  to trade); the **Luminaries**, an original pantheon of three (Solenne, Zephyrine, Thalor) who cannot be harmed,
  each in a temple structure in their own biome (Temple of Dawn, Spire of Winds, Arena of Stone, all `/locate`-able);
  three trials (an offering, a climb, and a boss fight against the **Avatar of Thalor**); `level.txt` stores
  `sift_trials` and `ascended`; passing all three plays the **Ascension** ending with a scrolling credits screen
  (`/credits`). **Divine** tool and armour tier (Divine Ingot, Soul Essence). **Blessings now work** (Light, Sky,
  Vigor) and so does **Health Boost**. The Sift has its own synthesised music and a deeper sky/fog.
  Script commands `talk`, `credits`; queries `trials`, `ascended`, `soultalks`, `eruptions`, `credits`, `screen`,
  `blessed`, `maxhp`. New tests `tests/blessings.txt`, `tests/sift.txt`.
- **Hell, expanded**: ghost variants per biome (**Ember Wraith**, **Fungal Shade**, **Cinder Specter**; all ghost rules now
  key on the GHOST AI rather than one mob); **Holy Water** (new `HOLY_WATER` projectile), **Blessed Sword**, soul
  lanterns (held or placed) repel and sear ghosts, **Tonic of Ghost Sight** (new `ghost_sight` effect: nearby ghosts
  glow and cannot phase) and **Phasing Pearl** (new `INSTANT` item use), both brewed from ectoplasm; the
  **Brimstone Revenant** mini-boss in a new arena at every Hell fortress's bridge crossing (boss bar, fire nova,

- **Sculk** (new `src/world/sculk.c`): vibrations from footsteps (not while sneaking), breaking (2) and placing (3)
  blocks and explosions (15); sculk sensors (16 power states) within 8 blocks turn on with power by distance
  (15 − ⌊d·15/8⌋), wool on the line absorbs vibrations, calibrated sensors only hear the frequency equal to the
  power fed into them, active sensors are redstone sources; shriekers (natural ones can summon) give Darkness and
  raise a saved warning level that decays every 10 minutes; at level 4 a warden emerges (`doWardenSpawning`);
  catalysts spread sculk (and rarely sensors/shriekers) where mobs die. Script `vibrate [freq]`.
- **Tripwire** (new `src/world/tripwire.c`): string between two hooks (≤ 40 blocks) on the same axis; anything in
  the string powers both hooks (strongly into their wall); breaking string trips it unless you use shears;
  shears disarm string (right-click or `activate`).
- **Pistons**: Minecraft-style push structures (12-block limit, slime and honey drag neighbours and don't stick to
  each other, immovable blocks cancel, fragile blocks break, sticky pistons pull whole slime structures, slime

- **Fishing**: the rod casts a bobber (new `BOBBER` projectile, fishing line drawn to the player) that floats,

  block/head, take it back with an empty hand, sneak-click for 6 poses). They break when their wall goes,
  `/summon item_frame|glow_item_frame|painting|armor_stand` works, and selectors know their types.
  Entity save format is now **UCE4** (adds pose and armour-stand equipment; UCE2/UCE3 still load).
- New tests: `guide`, `sculk` (21 checks), `tripwire` (10), `pistons` (12), `fishing` (13), `decor` (24).
- **Game rules that now work**: doEntityDrops, water/lavaSourceConversion, doVinesSpread, spawnerBlocksEnabled,

## v4 – "own world" update, part 1 (dimensions)
UltimateCraft starts becoming its own world: the Nether is now **Hell**, the End is now **Space**,
and a new fourth dimension, **the Sift** (heaven), is reached through a gold-and-glowstone gate.

- **Dimension registry** `src/world/dimension.{h,c}`: one table with each dimension's id, aliases,
  name, save folder, height, y offset, skylight, gravity and ambient light. Commands accept
  `overworld`, `hell` (`the_nether`, `nether`), `space` (`the_end`, `end`) and `sift` (`heaven`);
  `/execute in <dim>` now really switches dimension. Indices are unchanged (`DIM_SIFT` = 3 appended),
  so old `level.txt` files keep working. The Sift saves to `saves/<world>/DIM2/`.
- **Renames** (display names only; ids, saves and commands are unchanged): Nether → Hell,
  Netherrack → Hellrack, Netherite → Infernium, End Stone → Asteroid Stone, End Rod/Crystal/Portal →
  Star Rod / Star Crystal / Space Portal, Nether Fortress → Hell Fortress, End City → Star Citadel,
  Hell and Space biome names. F3 shows the new dimension ids and no longer prefixes biomes with `minecraft:`.
- **Hell – the Ghost**: a translucent flying undead (own model and skin, drawn in a new blended pass).
  It drifts toward players, phases through walls for 2–4 s at a time, drains health (healing itself)
  and inflicts **Haunted** (no natural regeneration, slow hunger drain). Holy sources – gold weapons,
  Smite, soul items, flaming arrows – deal double damage, everything else half; immune to fire.
  Drops **Ectoplasm**. Spawns in Soul Sand Valley, rarely elsewhere in Hell, and in Hell fortresses.
- **Space**: a full-sphere starfield with nebulae and a ringed planet; gravity 0.4× (higher, floatier
  jumps, softer landings); a vacuum that empties your air unless you wear the new **Space Helmet**
  (3 iron + 1 glass) or have the new **Oxygen** effect.
- **The Sift**: sky islands of Holy Stone and Golden Grass over a Cloud Sea (gaps fall into the void),
  Crystal Trees, Radiant Crystal, a guaranteed arrival island, eternal soft daylight, its own sky with a
  halo sun. Build a frame of gold blocks (glowstone corners optional) and use the **Sift Key** on it
  (Overworld only). Arrival builds a return portal; returning puts you at the gate you used.
  The key is currently `/give`-only; it will drop from the Space boss.
- New blocks: Cloud Block (no fall damage), Holy Stone, Holy Stone Bricks, Golden Grass Block, Crystal Log /
  Planks / Leaves, Radiant Crystal, Sift Portal. New items: Ectoplasm, Sift Key, Space Helmet, Ghost Spawn Egg.
  New effects: Haunted, Oxygen, Blessing of Light / of the Sky / of Vigor (blessings have no behaviour yet).
- **Save version 5**: `player.dat` stores its effect count, so new effects never break saves again
  (v4 saves are read with 40 effects, v3 with 27); `level.txt` gains `sift_return`.
- `/damage <target> <amount> <type> by <entity>` now uses the attacker; `player_attack`/`mob_attack` count as melee.
- `tools/gen_content.py` is now deterministic (two set iterations sorted), so regenerating no longer
  scrambles unrelated tables.
- Fixed: the noise-seed table only had 24 layers (out-of-bounds reads if more were used); now 32,
  with the first 24 unchanged so existing terrain is identical.
- Tests: new `tests/dimensions.txt` (25 checks) and `tests/hell.txt` (12 checks), both in `run_all.sh`;
  new `check` queries `near <block>,<r>`, `inbiome <biome>`, `phasing`, `ghostphases`, `mobhp <mob>`, `vely`, `siftret`.

fluids, redstone components, rails, pistons (6 facings), hoppers (5 facings), frosted ice, banner/spawner/
comparator/lectern block entities. Scheduled block updates are de-duplicated per tick. Shulker boxes keep
their contents (`saves/<w>/boxes.dat`). Stairs form inner/outer corners. Structure-only mobs were removed
from the biome spawn tables.

New tests: `tests/redstone.txt`, `features.txt`, `structures.txt`, `shulker.txt` (13 → 14 suites).

`recipebook`, `recipefill`, `trade`, `tradesel`, `profession`, `vehicle`, `mount`, `dismount`, `useitem`,

`named`, `layers`, `mapexplored`, `mapscale`, `prof`, `vxp`, `riding`, `moved`, `vehicles`, `instructure`,
